Meet The Owner

William Hoffman

Founder

Hi, I am the founder of Hoffman Remote Learning. I graduated summa cum laude from the University of Missouri with a Bachelor's degree in mathematics, and my career has consistently focused on helping students succeed in math.

I have extensive experience teaching and tutoring secondary students from 6th grade through high school. My background includes middle school math, advanced eighth-grade Pre-AP Algebra, and the full high school sequence of Algebra 1, Geometry, Algebra 2, Trigonometry, and Pre-Calculus.

My classroom experience includes five years of teaching in Dallas, Texas, and seven years in Missouri. After transitioning from full-time classroom instruction, I spent years remotely teaching and tutoring for school districts throughout the United States, partnering with various companies to supply quality instruction.

Today, as the owner of Hoffman Remote Learning, I bring that diverse background directly to your family—helping students master challenging concepts and prepare for whatever comes next.
